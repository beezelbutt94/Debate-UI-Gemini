# Product FAQ

## Billing
Invoices are issued monthly through Stripe. A failed card retries three times
over seven days before the subscription is cancelled. Refunds within 14 days
are handled by support on request.

## Checkout errors
A 402 during checkout means the Stripe payment intent was declined. A 500
during checkout is a bug and must be escalated to engineering with the
request ID from the error page.

## Accounts
Password resets are emailed within one minute. SSO is available on the Team
plan. Deleting an account removes all data after 30 days.
